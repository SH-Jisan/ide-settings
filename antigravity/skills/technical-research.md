# Technical Research

Use this skill when a technical decision depends on current information, technology comparison, ecosystem research, compatibility, standards, or external documentation.

## Define the Research Question

Identify:

* what needs to be researched,
* what decision it supports,
* relevant constraints,
* and required freshness.

Keep the research focused.

## Gather Reliable Sources

Prefer:

* official documentation,
* primary sources,
* recognized standards,
* maintained technical documentation,
* and reliable engineering resources.

Use community experience as supplementary evidence when practical experience is relevant.

## Verify Freshness

For time-sensitive information, verify:

* versions,
* support status,
* compatibility,
* publication or update dates,
* and important ecosystem changes.

## Compare Options

Evaluate options according to relevant:

* functionality,
* compatibility,
* security,
* performance,
* scalability,
* maintainability,
* learning curve,
* ecosystem,
* cost,
* and long-term sustainability.

## Recommend Practically

Clearly distinguish:

* verified facts,
* assumptions,
* trade-offs,
* and recommendations.

Recommend the option that best fits the user's actual scenario rather than simply selecting the newest or most popular option.
